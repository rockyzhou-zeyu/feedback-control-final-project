Two Clean Next Steps

Fuller Rigid-Body Model Add pitch, yaw, richer body segments, and front/rear load transfer.
Sidecut-Contact Model Replace the lumped grip law with board geometry, contact length, and snow sensitivity.

Why Not First More fidelity would have raised modeling and tuning cost before we had a clean baseline.

Talk Line
